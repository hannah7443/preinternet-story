Prior to the age of text messages, my mom kept a spiral-bound slam book — a private, analog notebook used to share juicy gossip, secrets, and thoughts amongst her friends.

Possessing the Slam Book

Possessing the notebook brought a rush of both excitement and caution. The notebook holder would have the opportunity to read the content written in it by the others, but would have to protect it at all costs.

A Replacement for Phones

Oftentimes, the notebook would be used as communication in place of phones: it was for information that didn't need to be said in the moment, and would perhaps be awkward or too confidential to be said aloud. The slam book was a playful yet effective way of communicating between friend groups in middle school in the '80s, according to my mom.
